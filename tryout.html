About Auschwitz

Auschwitz was the largest Nazi concentration and extermination camp during World War II, where over 1.1 million people were murdered. It remains a powerful symbol of the Holocaust and the consequences of hatred and intolerance.

Survivor Stories

Personal testimonies and accounts from survivors bring human faces to the history and remind us of the resilience of the human spirit. These stories are vital to remembering and educating future generations.
